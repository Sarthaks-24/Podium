You are given $n$ integers. Put them in increasing order and look at every two neighbours. Print the largest difference between two neighbours.

## Input

The first line holds $n$. The second line holds the $n$ integers, separated by single spaces.

## Output

Print one integer: the largest difference between two neighbouring values after sorting.

## Constraints

- $2 \le n \le 200\,000$
- Subtask 1 (40 points): $n \le 100$ and $|x_i| \le 1000$
- Subtask 2 (60 points): $n \le 200\,000$ and $|x_i| \le 10^9$

The answer can exceed the range of a 32-bit integer.
