podium is the contest site the organisers run for weekly programming contests. This page says what it records about you, who can see it, and how long it is kept. It describes the site as it works today.

## What podium records

- **Your account.** Your email address, your name and your profile picture address as Google sends them when you sign in, the role the organisers gave you, and when you last signed in. podium keeps Google's account number for you so that nobody else can sign in with your address.
- **Your sign-in sessions.** When each session started, the network address it came from and your browser's name, so the organisers can end a session that is not yours.
- **Your code.** Everything you run or submit: the code, the language, when you sent it, the verdict and the score. The editor also keeps a draft of your code for each problem so it is still there after a reload.
- **How you wrote it, during a contest.** For each submission, how many characters you typed and how many you pasted. While a contest is open, podium also records when you paste (the length, not the text), when you leave the page and come back, and when your account is in the contest from two places at once. Outside contests none of this is recorded.
- **Your questions and messages.** Questions you ask during a contest and their answers, and messages you send with "Write to the organisers".
- **Your result in each contest.** Once a contest's results are final, podium keeps a summary for you: your score, your share of the points, your place, and your score, attempts and best result on each problem. "Your progress" is built from these summaries. Nothing is written while a contest is running.
- **Notices.** Messages podium sends you: that a contest's results are final, that an organiser asked about a submission, a reminder, a decision, or a change to your standing. A notice says what happened and what to do; it never holds code, test data or anyone's name. podium records when you read it. Notices wait while you are in a contest and arrive when it ends.
- **Organisers' private notes.** An organiser can write a note on one of your submissions, for example the lines that need a closer look. You are not told about a note. If an organiser opens a question from it, you see the question and the lines in question, never the note's text.
- **Questions about your work, and decisions.** When an organiser asks you to explain a submission: the question, your explanation (every version you save), the decision and its reason, and any appeal you send with its ruling.
- **Your standing.** A short record of decisions about you: each confirmed outcome and each adjustment by an organiser, with the date and the reason written for you. You see it as a band ("Good standing", "Outcomes on record" or "Repeated outcomes") and the dated lines on your Questions and standing page.
- **A rating, for the organisers only.** Behind the band, the organisers keep a rating from 0 to 10 and every change to it: what caused it, the reason, who made it and who confirmed it. You never see the number; you see the band and each dated line, and you can appeal every line.
- **Who read a student's record.** Each time an organiser reads your record, a question about you or the notes on your submissions, podium records who read it and when.
- **Your preferences.** The theme and verdict colours you picked, and whether you chose to hide your progress page.

podium does not record the keys you press outside the editor, your screen, your camera or anything outside its own page.

## Who can see it

- **You** see your own submissions, scores, questions, messages, notices, your progress page, your standing with its reasons, and your appeals with their rulings.
- **Other students** see your name, your place and your scores on a contest's standings, and nothing else. They never see your progress, your standing, your notices or any question about your work.
- **The organisers** (owners and admins, and co-organisers for their own contests) see everything listed above for the contests they run. The paste and focus records are shown to them on a contest's integrity screens.
- **Owners and admins** also see your progress page, your record (standing, rating, questions, notes and appeals) and your results across contests. A co-organiser who is a student, such as a teaching assistant, never sees notes, questions, standing or the rating.
- **Only the owner** sees the record of who read a student's record.
- **Google** handles your sign-in. podium sends nothing else to Google.
- Your code is judged on podium's own server, in a sandbox. It is never sent to an AI service. Organisers may use an AI service to draft problems; that uses the problem text only, never anything about you.

Your progress page is never public. Hiding it, from the account menu, empties the page for you; the summaries are kept, and the organisers still see them.

## How long it is kept

<!-- retention -->

When a period ends, the server deletes the data itself, every day. Your scores in past contests stay on their standings.

## Your choices

You can ask the organisers for a copy of what podium holds about you, or to remove your account. Removing it deletes your sessions, drafts, paste and focus records, messages, notices and contest summaries, and replaces your name and email with "Removed user" everywhere. Your submissions and scores stay, so past standings do not change. Notes, questions, decisions, appeals, your standing and the rating stay until their own period ends, because they are the organisers' record of a decision.

You can appeal any outcome or adjustment on your standing within 14 days of it. An organiser who took no part in the decision rules on the appeal and tells you the reason.
