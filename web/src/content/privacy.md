podium is the contest site the organisers run for programming contests. This page says what it records about you, who can see it, and how long it is kept. It describes the site as it works today.

## What podium records

- **Your account.** Your email address, your name and your profile picture address as Google sends them when you sign in, the role the organisers gave you, when you joined and when you last signed in. podium keeps Google's account number for you so that nobody else can sign in with your address.
- **Your sign-in sessions.** When each session started, the network address it came from and your browser's name, so the organisers can end a session that is not yours.
- **Your code.** Everything you submit: the code, the language, when you sent it, the verdict and the score. When you only run your code, podium keeps a fingerprint of the code, not the code, and the input and output of that run for a short time. The editor saves a draft of your code for each problem on the server and in your browser, so it is still there after a reload or on another computer.
- **How you wrote it, during a contest.** For each submission, how many characters you typed and how many you pasted. While your contest is open, podium also records when you paste (the length, never the text), when you leave the page and come back, when you leave full screen, and when your account is open in two places at once. For that last check your browser keeps a random device number, and podium keeps a fingerprint of your network address and browser. Outside contests none of this is recorded.
- **Your contests.** When you started each contest and accepted its rules, any extra time an organiser gave you and the reason they wrote for it.
- **Your questions.** Questions you ask during a contest and their answers.
- **Similar-code checks.** After a contest, an organiser can compare everyone's submissions to find code that is very alike. podium keeps what the check found and any decision an organiser records about it (no action, a warning, or excluded from the standings), with the reason.
- **Your preferences.** The theme and verdict colours you picked are kept with your account. Editor settings stay in your browser only.

podium does not record the keys you press, your screen, your camera, or anything outside its own page.

## Who can see it

- **You** see your own submissions, runs, drafts and questions.
- **Other students** see your name, your place and your scores on a contest's standings, never your email. If you ask a question and the organisers answer it for everyone, others see the question and the answer, not who asked.
- **Organisers and owners** see everything listed above for every contest, including your email, your code, and the paste, focus and full-screen records on a contest's similar-code screens.
- **Only owners** manage accounts and roles, and see the record of what was done to an account.
- **Google** handles your sign-in, which asks only for your name, email and picture. podium sends nothing else to Google.
- Your code is judged on podium's own server, in a sandbox. It is never sent to an AI service. Organisers may use an AI service to draft problems; that uses the text they type, never anything about you.

## How long it is kept

<!-- retention -->

When a period ends, the server deletes the data itself. Submissions, scores, your code, decisions about similar code and the record of what organisers did are kept, so past standings do not change.

## Your choices

You can ask the organisers for a copy of what podium holds about you, or to remove your account. Removing it deletes your sessions, drafts and paste and focus records, clears the reasons for any extra time and the text of your questions, and replaces your name and email with "Removed user" everywhere. Your submissions and scores stay, so past standings do not change. Backups that hold your data are deleted when their own period ends.
