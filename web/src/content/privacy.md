podium is the contest site the organisers run for weekly programming contests. This page says what it records about you, who can see it, and how long it is kept. It describes the site as it works today.

## What podium records

- **Your account.** Your email address, your name and your profile picture address as Google sends them when you sign in, the role the organisers gave you, and when you last signed in. podium keeps Google's account number for you so that nobody else can sign in with your address.
- **Your sign-in sessions.** When each session started, the network address it came from and your browser's name, so the organisers can end a session that is not yours.
- **Your code.** Everything you run or submit: the code, the language, when you sent it, the verdict and the score. The editor also keeps a draft of your code for each problem so it is still there after a reload.
- **How you wrote it, during a contest.** For each submission, how many characters you typed and how many you pasted. While a contest is open, podium also records when you paste (the length, not the text), when you leave the page and come back, and when your account is in the contest from two places at once. Outside contests none of this is recorded.
- **Your questions and messages.** Questions you ask during a contest and their answers, and messages you send with "Write to the organisers".
- **Your preferences.** The theme and verdict colours you picked.

podium does not record the keys you press outside the editor, your screen, your camera or anything outside its own page.

## Who can see it

- **You** see your own submissions, scores, questions and messages.
- **Other students** see your name, your place and your scores on a contest's standings, and nothing else.
- **The organisers** (owners and admins, and co-organisers for their own contests) see everything listed above for the contests they run. The paste and focus records are shown to them on a contest's integrity screens.
- **Google** handles your sign-in. podium sends nothing else to Google.
- Your code is judged on podium's own server, in a sandbox. It is never sent to an AI service. Organisers may use an AI service to draft problems; that uses the problem text only, never anything about you.

## How long it is kept

<!-- retention -->

When a period ends, the server deletes the data itself, every day. Your scores in past contests stay on their standings.

## Your choices

You can ask the organisers for a copy of what podium holds about you, or to remove your account. Removing it deletes your sessions, drafts, paste and focus records and messages, and replaces your name and email with "Removed user" everywhere. Your submissions and scores stay, so past standings do not change.
